  1. Match Setup: Enter your battle name and click FIND MATCH to search for an opponent.
  2. Deck Draft: Draft exactly 5 cards to form your battle deck. You must ensure you choose at least 4 unique elements!
  3. Quests: Select 1 Quest at the start of each round. Completing the requirements during the battle round unlocks a choice of passive relics.
  4. Card Battles: Play 1 card each round. The cards clash based on element matchups (see MATCHUPS tab):
    • SUPERIOR: Your card counters the opponent's card. Your card's Superior outcomes trigger.
    • NEUTRAL: The clash is a tie. Neutral outcomes trigger.
    • INFERIOR: Your card is countered. Inferior outcomes trigger.
  5. Status Effects:
    • BURN: Deals damage equal to 3x stack size at round end. Absorbed by shields first. Stacks consume after ticking.
    • POISON: Deals damage equal to stack size at start of round, bypassing shields. Ticks down by 1 stack per round.
    • BUFF: Increases all damage dealt by the buff stack size.
    • WEAKNESS: Reduces damage dealt by 2 per stack (minimum 0).
  6. Quest Rewards & Relics: Passive relics stack and provide permanent passive boosts (e.g. increasing Max HP, starting Shield, card removal).
  7. Winning: Reduce your opponent's HP to 0 to win the duel!
