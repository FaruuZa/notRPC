CARD CLASH OUTCOMES

SUPERIOR OUTCOME

Your card element counters the opponent's element.

  • Triggers all Superior damage and status effects
  • Opponent card is suppressed to Inferior
NEUTRAL OUTCOME

Both cards clash in equal standing or identical element (Tie).

  • Both cards trigger standard Neutral effects
  • Base damage and neutral shields apply symmetrically
INFERIOR OUTCOME

Your card element is countered by the opponent.

  • Triggers Inferior mitigation or recoil effects
  • Mitigates defeat and primes comeback buffs
TACTICAL STATUSES & KEYWORDS

STATUS EFFECTS & COMBAT KEYWORDS

BURN FIRE DEBUFF

Deals damage equal to 3x stack size at round end. Absorbed by shields first. Stacks are fully consumed after ticking.

POISON NATURE DEBUFF

Deals direct damage equal to 1x stack size at round end, bypassing shields completely. Decreases by 1 stack per round.

ATTACK BUFF AMPLIFIER

Increases card clash damage by +10% per stack. Decreases by 1 stack when attacking.

WEAKNESS SUPPRESSION

Reduces card clash damage dealt by -10% per stack. Decreases by 1 stack when attacking.

SHIELD PROTECTION

Blocks incoming clash & burn damage. Decays by 50% at round end (surviving shield carries over).

OUTBREAK CATALYST

Activates before round ticks, instantly doubling current Poison stacks on target (doubled stacks take effect starting next turn).

LEECH DRAIN

When your card clash deals unblocked HP damage to an enemy, restores 20% of that damage per stack (uncapped). Stack consumed only on trigger.

THORNS RETALIATION

Deals retaliation damage back to attacker whenever hit by enemy card clash (even if blocked by Shield). Damages Shield first before HP. Consumes 1 stack per trigger.

CLEANSE PURIFY

Instantly purges all active debuffs (Poison, Burn, Weakness) from self.

DISPEL NULLIFY

Instantly strips all positive buffs and status advantages from the opposing combatant.
