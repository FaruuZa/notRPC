  1. Match Setup: Enter your battle name and click FIND MATCH to search for an opponent.
  2. Deck Draft: Draft exactly 5 cards to form your battle deck. You must ensure you choose at least 4 unique elements!
  3. Synchronized Quests: Select 1 Quest at the start of each round. Both players receive the exact same rarity distribution each round. Completing quests unlocks main rewards (Relic or Bonus Draft) plus guaranteed Removal Tokens (Common: +0, Rare: +1, Epic: +2).
  4. Card Battles: Play 1 card each round. The cards clash based on element matchups (see MATCHUPS tab):
    • SUPERIOR: Your card counters the opponent's card. Your card's Superior outcomes trigger.
    • NEUTRAL: The clash is a tie. Neutral outcomes trigger.
    • INFERIOR: Your card is countered. Inferior outcomes trigger.
  5. Status Effects & Combat Keywords:
    • BURN: Deals damage equal to 3x stack size at round end. Absorbed by shields first. Stacks are fully consumed after ticking.
    • POISON: Deals direct damage equal to stack size at round start, bypassing shields completely. Decreases by 1 stack per round.
    • BUFF: Increases card clash damage by +10% per stack. Decreases by 1 stack when attacking.
    • WEAKNESS: Reduces card clash damage dealt by -10% per stack. Decreases by 1 stack when attacking.
    • SHIELD: Blocks incoming clash & burn damage. Decays by 50% at round end (surviving shield carries over).
    • OUTBREAK: Instantly doubles all active Poison stacks on target (amplifier only; unpoisoned target receives 0).
    • LEECH: When dealing direct unblocked HP damage, restores 20% HP per stack of damage dealt (capped at 10 HP per hit) and consumes 1 stack.
    • THORN: When taking direct clash HP damage, reflects damage equal to current stack back to attacker (bypasses shield) and consumes 1 stack.
    • CLEANSE: Instantly purges all active debuffs (Poison, Burn, Weakness) from self.
    • DISPEL: Instantly strips all positive buffs from the opponent.
  6. Deck Purification & Tokens: Unused Removal Tokens persist in your inventory across rounds. During Preparation Phase, spend 1 token to permanently burn an unwanted card from your deck (minimum deck size is 4 cards).
  7. Winning: Reach 3 round points or reduce your opponent's HP to 0 to win the duel!
